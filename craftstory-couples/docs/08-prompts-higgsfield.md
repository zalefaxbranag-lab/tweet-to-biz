# Les 12 prompts — Higgsfield (images puis Seedance 2.5)

6 images de départ, 6 vidéos. Tout en **16:9**, 1080p, Bitrate High, audio On
côté vidéo. Chaque vidéo part de l'image du même numéro passée en Reference.

Trois clauses reviennent partout, et ce sont elles qui font la différence :

- **bandeau** — le montage recadre en 1920×540 (32:9), donc rien d'important
  dans le cinquième haut ni le cinquième bas du cadre ;
- **regard** — ils fixent un écran situé juste sous l'axe de la caméra, jamais
  l'objectif : c'est ce qui crée la position de spectateur ;
- **anti-split** — le split final est fait au montage, jamais par le modèle.

Wide two-shot, locked-off tripod framing. Both faces on the same horizontal line, centred
vertically, with generous empty headroom above and empty floor below that can be cropped
away. Nothing important in the top fifth or the bottom fifth of the frame.

Photorealistic editorial photograph, full-frame camera, 35mm lens at f/2, realistic skin
texture with visible pores and fine lines, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, warm
filmic colour grade. Not an illustration, not a render, not AI-looking, no plastic skin,
no over-smoothing, no waxy highlights.

One single full-frame image, no split screen, no second panel, no borders, no collage, no
duplicate, no text, no watermark, no logo, no subtitles.

## VIDÉO 1A — réaction, les mariés · joie incrédule qui bascule

```
Use the reference image as the exact starting frame: same couple, same sofa, same room,
same screen light. Do not change the framing, the lens or the lighting. The camera stays
locked off on a tripod for the whole shot, with a barely perceptible slow push in. They
keep looking at the screen below the camera and never look into the lens.

They are watching a music video made about them for the first time.

0-1s: both settled, half-smiling, expecting something ordinary, her shoulders relaxed.
1-3s: the song starts. Her smile changes shape, the corners pulling down before they go
up. He turns his head to look at her instead of the screen.
3-5s: she brings one hand up over her mouth. Her breath catches and her chest lifts
sharply once. Her eyes fill but nothing falls yet.
5-7s: he pulls her into him. She laughs, wet and broken, and the laugh cracks in the
middle.
7-9s: one tear runs and she does not wipe it. He kisses the top of her head, still
watching the screen over her.
9-10s: both go quiet, holding each other, the screen light shifting across their faces.

Audio: no dialogue and no words. A sharp intake of breath around 3s, a broken wet laugh at
5-6s, one shaky exhale at 8s, and room tone. Faint muffled music as if leaking from laptop
speakers, never clear enough to follow.

Photorealistic, full-frame camera, 35mm lens at f/2, lit only by the screen and one warm
lamp behind them, realistic skin with visible pores, tears catching the screen light,
subtle film grain. Not an illustration, not a render, no plastic skin, no over-smoothing.

Keep both faces on the same horizontal line, centred vertically, with empty headroom above
and floor below. No split screen, no second panel, no overlay, no text, no watermark, no
logo, no subtitles. One single continuous take, no cut.
```
