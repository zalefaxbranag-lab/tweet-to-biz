# Les 6 prompts corrigés — format vertical mesuré

Remplace `08-prompts-higgsfield.md`, écrit avant d'avoir vu la créa. Les
changements viennent des mesures de `09-format-reel-mesure.md`, pas du goût.

**Réglages :** ratio **1:1** pour les deux moitiés (les bandeaux mesurés font
1,10 et 1,15), 1080p, Bitrate High, 10 s. **Audio : peu importe** — il est
jeté au montage et remplacé par la chanson.

Ce qui change par rapport à la v1 :

| v1 (faux) | v2 (mesuré) |
|---|---|
| paysage 16:9, bandeaux 32:9 | **vertical, bandeaux quasi carrés → générer en 1:1** |
| trépied verrouillé, lent push in | **proche, à la main, le cadre se recompose, des membres traversent l'objectif** |
| 6 battements en 10 s | **3 battements** |
| larme, main sur la bouche, chagrin retenu | **joie qui déborde, qui se résout en un geste vers l'autre** |
| consignes audio seconde par seconde | **aucune : la chanson est posée au montage** |
| clip musical très animé | **quasi statique, un plan tenu puis un changement** |

## 1A — réaction, les mariés

```
Use the reference image as the first frame. Square frame. Handheld, as if filmed on a
phone propped on the coffee table: the frame drifts and recomposes, it is never locked
off. Close two-shot, their heads filling most of the frame. They keep looking at the
screen below the camera and never look into the lens.

Three beats in ten seconds, no more:

1. She sees what it is. Mouth open, eyebrows up, she grabs his arm without looking at him.
2. It breaks open. She throws both hands up past the top of the frame and lifts off the
sofa, laughing out loud, her arm crossing the lens for a moment. He is laughing at her,
not at the screen.
3. She turns away from the screen and throws both arms around his neck, face buried in his
shoulder, still laughing. He holds the back of her head and rocks her once. They stay
there.

Photorealistic, lit only by the screen in front of them and one warm lamp behind, realistic
skin with visible pores, natural motion blur on the fast movements, subtle grain. Not an
illustration, not a render, no plastic skin, no over-smoothing, no slow motion.

One single continuous take, no cut. No split screen, no second panel, no overlay, no text,
no watermark, no logo, no subtitles.
```

## 2A — réaction, la quarantaine

Même énergie, registre différent : le geste est vers l'autre, mais plus lent
et plus lourd. Ils ne sautent pas, ils s'effondrent l'un sur l'autre.

```
Use the reference image as the first frame. Square frame. Handheld, the frame drifting and
recomposing as if filmed on a phone leaning against something on the table. Close two-shot.
They keep looking at the small screen below the camera and never look into the lens.

Three beats in ten seconds, no more:

1. He recognises something and says nothing. His hand goes flat on the table. She has not
reacted yet.
2. She puts both hands over her face, then drops them, laughing and shaking her head at
the screen, eyes wet. He is watching her now.
3. He pulls her sideways into him with one arm, hard enough that she has to catch herself
on the table, and she laughs into his shoulder. He kisses the side of her head and they
stay leaning together.

Photorealistic, lit only by the phone screen and a single hanging bulb over the table,
realistic skin with visible pores and fine lines, natural motion blur, subtle grain. Not an
illustration, not a render, no plastic skin, no over-smoothing, no slow motion.

One single continuous take, no cut. No split screen, no second panel, no overlay, no text,
no watermark, no logo, no subtitles.
```

## 3A — réaction, les soixante-dix ans

Le geste est minuscule et c'est ce qui le rend fort. On garde les trois
battements, mais l'explosion est intérieure.

```
Use the reference image as the first frame. Square frame. Handheld, the frame drifting
slightly as if filmed by someone standing just behind the tablet. Close two-shot. They keep
looking at the screen below the camera and never look into the lens.

Three beats in ten seconds, no more:

1. Both go completely still. Her mouth opens very slightly.
2. His chin drops and his eyes close hard. His hand comes up and covers hers on his
forearm, gripping it.
3. She turns and presses her forehead against his temple, eyes still closed, and he laughs
one silent breath and does not move. They stay like that, tears on both faces, neither of
them wiping anything.

Photorealistic, lit only by the tablet screen and an amber lamp behind them, realistic aged
skin with deep lines and age spots, visible pores, tears catching the screen light, subtle
grain. Not an illustration, not a render, no plastic skin, no over-smoothing, no slow
motion.

One single continuous take, no cut. No split screen, no second panel, no overlay, no text,
no watermark, no logo, no subtitles.
```
